Explore Jordan's history, nature, culture, and landmarks in Arabic, English, and Japanese.

Visit the website: [Jordan Travel Guide](https://hussamoalmasre2009-source.github.io/jordan-story/)

The site supports Arabic, English, Japanese, Russian, and Latin, with a separate language button for each. It includes flexible three-, five-, and seven-day route ideas, map links, and official travel-planning resources; opening hours, prices, reservations, and entry requirements should be checked with the relevant authorities before travel. Ratings are handed off to WhatsApp for the visitor to review and send; they are not submitted or stored by the website.

The Jordan game is a side-scrolling platform adventure with six destinations, region-specific scenery inspired by local landmarks, running and jumping controls, collectible artifacts, scorpions, touch controls, optional sound, and increasing difficulty.
